🛡️ How Resend Rate Limiting Protects Your App ▼

OTP resend buttons are the #1 attack surface in modern authentication. Without strict limiting, malicious actors exploit them for:

  • Email Bombing: Flooding victim inboxes with hundreds of unsolicited OTP emails.
  • API Toll Fraud: Depleting your email provider quota (SendGrid, Postmark, Resend).
  • Brute-Force Exploits: Generating excessive codes to widen brute-force windows.
Two-Tier Defense Implemented in this Example:
  • Tier 1 (Client Cooldown): A 60-second reactive timer disables the UI button immediately upon sending.
  • Tier 2 (Server Middleware): DevAuthKit's sliding-window rate limiter enforces per-email limits (max 3/min) and per-IP limits (max 10/min), returning HTTP 429 Too Many Requests with Retry-After headers.
